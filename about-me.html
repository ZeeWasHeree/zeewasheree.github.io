About Me

Physics Education Student at Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta

Hi everyone! My name is I'am Erian Mu'izz. You can just call me "I'am" (pronounced [ik.am] or /iʔ.am/) or "Iam" (pronounced [i.yam] or /i.jam/) instead. I'm a first-year student that was born in Banyuwangi, a city right on the eastern tip of Java Island. Currently, I'm pursuing my studies at Yogyakarta State University (or Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta) in the Faculty of Mathematics and Natural Sciences, where I'm majoring in Physics Education.

When I have some free time, you'll probably find me exploring Teyvat in Genshin Impact, climbing the ranks in Mobile Legends, or even playing a game of chess! I'll admit that I'm not the greatest gamer out there, but playing games actually sharpens my decision-making skills and trains my mind to think more logically and deeply.

Looking ahead, my main goal in life is to keep growing as a person and eventually be useful for the society. Because of that, I was determined to become a teacher one day. In the meantime, I am currently freelancing as a Manhwa comic translator, adapting stories from English into Indonesian. I chose this path because, as I mentioned, I genuinely want to be helpful and make life a bit easier for others.

I'm also a big believer in efficiency for everything I do. That's why I frequently create Python scripts or use ADB automation to simplify my tasks and optimize my workflow. I really enjoy streamlining processes because it allows me to consistently deliver much better results in the future.

My coding journey actually started back in early 2020 when the pandemic closed down schools and kept me at home. My very first project was building a Discord bot for my Discord store using the Discord.js library. That taught me a lot of things about Node.js, and from there, I gradually transitioned into learning Python little by little through Discord.py.

When it comes to getting work done, I'm definitely a night owl. I usually did my tasks and projects in the middle of the night because that's when my focus is at its absolute peak. I love the quiet of the night since there are no distracting noises to disrupt my flow. As a result, I tend to go to bed pretty late. Sometimes 1 a.m., sometimes 2 a.m., or occasionally I don't sleep at all! But don't worry, I can still function perfectly smoothly the next day.

Aside from my passion for coding, I'll be completely honest with you. I really love money. That's why I always push myself to finish my work as quickly as possible with the best results. To me, excellent results combined with speed equal more money, since customers are more likely to hire me again.
